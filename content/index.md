> _Your recipes. Your vault. Your kitchen._

Recipe Box is an [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) plugin that turns your recipe notes into a complete meal planning, grocery shopping, and recipe management system - all stored as plain Markdown in your vault.

[[#Where to Start]] | [[#Feature Overview]]

![[System/Attachments/device-mockup.png|643]]

## Everything Lives in Markdown

Recipe Box uses regular Obsidian notes for everything it manages.

That means your data stays future-proof, your recipes are searchable, you can query and display them with [Dataview](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/dataview) or [Obsidian Bases](https://obsidian.md/help/bases), they work with backlinks and your existing vault structure, and you can always access your data without the plugin.

Your recipes belong to you.

## Feature Overview

### Manage and View Your Recipe Notes

- Specify a vault folder and all recipes within it will be converted managed by the plugin
- View your recipes using desktop and mobile-optimized layouts, including swipe navigation between tabs
- Health & safety warnings for meat temperatures, allergens, and high glycemic-index ingredients
- Add custom frontmatter properties to the recipe view

### Plan Your Meals

- Add recipes to a meal plan and select a day and meal type, or add to a queue to decide later
- Generate a grocery list from only what you need when adding to the meal plan
- Drag and drop recipes in and out of the queue and between days

### Build Your Grocery List

- Use the shopping assistant to group your grocery items by category, recipe, source, or a flat list
- Auto-add ingredients to the grocery list when recipes are added to the meal plan

### Start Cooking

- Meal suggestions based on favorites, or how recently you've cooked something. Or build your own custom recipe suggester!
- Scale recipes up or down with a multiplier
- Built-in kitchen timers detected from your instructions
- Track cook history per recipe, with dates, notes, and photos - stored in frontmatter for querying with [Dataview](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/dataview) or [Obsidian Bases](https://obsidian.md/help/bases)
- Cook mode to keep your screen awake while cooking
